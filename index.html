Project summary · resumen en inglés

Laser spy is our 7th-grade robotics project. A laser points at a light sensor; if something breaks the beam, the Arduino sounds an alarm. The site has six parts: four for the presentation (01 Project, 02 Materials, 03 Development and 06 Team) and two support modules (04 Specifications and 05 Assembly).
